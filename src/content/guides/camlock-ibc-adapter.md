# Camlock IBC Adapters: Connecting 2" Tote Valves to Hoses and Transfer Lines

IBC totes (1000 L intermediate bulk containers) are everywhere in chemical, food and oil transfer — and the standard way to get liquid out of a tote is a camlock on the valve outlet. The 2" butterfly valve on most totes is not camlock-compatible out of the box; you need the right adapter. Here is how to spec camlock IBC adapters correctly.

## The tote valve outlet

Most IBC totes ship with a **2" butterfly valve**, and the valve outlet thread is the first thing to identify:

- **2" BSP / buttress (S60) thread** — the most common IBC valve outlet thread (buttress is the trapezoidal thread used on tote valves)
- **2" NPT** — common on North American totes and aftermarket valves
- **2" female NPT or BSP** — some valves have female outlets that accept a male adapter directly

Measure or read the valve stamp before ordering — **BSP and NPT are not interchangeable**, and a mismatch is a leak or a cross-threaded valve.

## The adapter types

| Adapter | Valve side | Hose side | Use |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 2" female NPT × Type A (male camlock) | threads onto the male valve outlet | male camlock for a Type C/D/DC coupler | The standard tote-to-hose adapter |
| 2" male NPT/BSP × Type C (male hose shank) | threads into a female valve outlet | hose shank for hose clamps | Female-outlet totes |
| IBC-specific adapters (buttress × camlock) | buttress thread mating the valve | camlock | Direct tote connection without a valve in between |
| Camlock reducer / bushing sets | — | — | Stepping 2" tote flow down to 1" or 1½" hoses |

Most transfer rigs run the **2" female NPT × Type A adapter** into the tote valve, then a **Type C or D coupler** on the hose — the standard A+C pump-to-hose layout, now seated on the tote.

## Material rules by tote contents

- **Chemical totes** → **polypropylene** is the standard: chemically inert, lightweight, no corrosion. PP camlocks and PP adapters are the chemical-industry default for acids, alkalis and solvents (check the specific chemical compatibility for gasket choice)
- **Food totes** (syrup, juice, edible oil) → **stainless steel** with food-grade EPDM or silicone gaskets
- **Water and oil totes** → **aluminum** works for clean water and most oils; brass for petroleum products where spark resistance matters

## Gasket selection for IBC transfer

- **EPDM** — water, most acids and food products
- **Viton (FKM)** — aggressive solvents, fuels, high-temperature chemicals
- **NBR** — oil and fuel service
- **PTFE** — maximum chemical inertness on the nastiest duty

When in doubt on a chemical tote, ask for a compatibility check before the adapter goes into service — a gasket failure on a chemical line is a spill, not a drip.

## Transfer layouts

- **Tote to hose (chemical)**: 2" female NPT × Type A adapter on the valve → Type C coupler → chemical hose → pump → destination
- **Tote to process (food)**: stainless adapter + food-grade gasket → 3/4" or 1" line for controlled dosing, or 2" for bulk transfer
- **Gravity flow**: open the butterfly valve fully — a half-open valve restricts flow and starves the line
- **Multiple totes**: manifold totes with camlock T/ball-valve manifolds for continuous feed

## Handling notes

1. **Support the hose** — a heavy chemical hose hanging off the tote valve stresses the adapter and the valve; support the hose run
2. **Close the butterfly valve before uncoupling** — then bleed any line pressure before opening the cam arms
3. **Cap the adapter when idle** — dust caps and plugs keep the thread and groove clean
4. **Check the valve gasket** — tote valve gaskets fail before camlock gaskets on many rigs; inspect both
5. **No gas service** — camlocks and IBC adapters are liquid-service; never pressurize a tote with gas

## Checklist for an IBC adapter order

- Valve outlet: identify **2" BSP buttress vs 2" NPT** first
- Adapter: 2" female NPT × Type A (standard), buttress × camlock (direct), or reducer set
- Material: PP (chemical), stainless (food), aluminum/brass (water/oil)
- Gasket: EPDM / Viton / NBR / PTFE per the contents
- Extras: dust caps, spare gaskets, a camlock hose coupler set (C + D) to complete the rig
