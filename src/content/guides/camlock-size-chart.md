# Camlock Size Chart: Measure Once, Order Right

Camlock sizes describe the **nominal bore** of the hose or pipe the coupling fits — not the outside diameter of the fitting, and not the outside of the hose. A 2" camlock clamps onto 2" hose. That's the whole trick, and it's where most order mix-ups start.

## Sizes Available

Standard cam and groove couplings are made in these nominal sizes:

| Size | Typical duty |
|:--|:--|
| 1/2" | Small lines, sampling, laboratory |
| 3/4" | Pilot plants, small pumps |
| 1" | Transfer skids, small pumps |
| 1-1/4" | Irrigation sub-mains |
| 1-1/2" | General transfer, fire reels |
| 2" | **The workhorse** — pumps, tankers, irrigation |
| 2-1/2" | Larger pumps, tanker offload |
| 3" | Tanker loading, dewatering |
| 4" | Bulk transfer, manifolds |
| 6" | Large bore bulk / dredging |

Aluminum and stainless are stocked 1/2" through 6". Polypropylene runs 1/2" through 4". Brass runs 1/2" through 4".

## The 60-Second Measuring Method

1. **Measure the hose bore** (inside diameter) in inches. Most transfer hose is labelled — 2", 3", etc. Trust the label, verify with a ruler.
2. **For threaded ends**, measure the thread: NPT vs BSPT. NPT is tapered, BSPT is parallel with a taper — they are NOT interchangeable. When in doubt, send us a photo of the thread end.
3. **Never mix sizes.** A 2" adapter will not seat in a 2-1/2" coupler. The cams won't reach the groove, and forcing it bends the arms.

## Pairing Rules in One Line

**Male adapters (A, C, E) mate with female couplers (B, D, F) of the same size.** Same size, always. DC dust caps cap female ends; DP dust plugs plug male ends.

## Metric Gotchas

Camlocks are an inch-world product. A "50 mm" coupling is usually the 2" size (50.8 mm nominal bore) — confirm the inch size before ordering metric hose. We also stock 25 mm and 63 mm variants on request.

## FAQ

**Is a 2" camlock the same as 2" hose?**
Yes — nominal bore matches. If your hose is labelled 2", order 2" camlocks.

**How do I know if my existing camlock is 1-1/2" or 2"?**
Measure the inside of the female coupler or the groove end of the male adapter. A 2" bore is roughly 50 mm across; 1-1/2" is about 38 mm.

**Can I step between sizes?**
Yes — camlock reducers connect a larger coupling to a smaller hose. Size the coupling to the larger bore and the reducer steps it down.
