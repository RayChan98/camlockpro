## FAQ

**Why choose brass over aluminum?**

Brass handles higher thread strength, better spark resistance in fuel/gas environments, and repeated connect-disconnect cycles without galling.

**Which threads?**

NPT (US) and BSPT (UK/EU) are both stocked. Confirm your standard before ordering — they are not interchangeable.

**Can brass camlocks handle gasoline?**

Yes. Brass with NBR gaskets is the standard combination for gasoline, diesel and lube oil transfer.
