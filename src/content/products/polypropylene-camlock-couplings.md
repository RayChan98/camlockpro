## FAQ

**What chemicals can PP camlocks handle?**

PP resists most acids, alkalis, solvents and deionized water at ambient temperature. For hot or concentrated chemicals, check the compatibility chart or specify PTFE gaskets.

**Are PP camlocks food safe?**

Yes, PP with food-grade EPDM gaskets is used in food plants, breweries and potable water systems.

**Why choose PP over metal?**

Half the weight of aluminum, zero corrosion, no galvanic issues, and lower cost — ideal for chemical plants and mobile transfer.
