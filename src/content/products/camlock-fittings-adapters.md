# Camlock Fittings & Adapters

Threaded adapters, reducers, hose shanks, elbows and flanges that connect camlock couplings to pipes, hoses, tanks and IBC totes.

## Specifications

| Fitting type | Connection ends | Typical use |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Threaded adapter (A/B) | Camlock × NPT/BSPT | Pipe, pump and equipment connections |
| Hose shank (C/E) | Camlock × barbed hose | Hose-end connections with clamps |
| Reducer | Two camlock sizes | Stepping between hose/pipe sizes |
| Flange adapter | Camlock × ANSI flange | Tank and manifold connections |
| Elbow (45°/90°) | Camlock × camlock | Tight-space routing |

**Standard:** A-A-59326D (formerly MIL-C-27487) profile — interchangeable with Dixon, Banjo, PT and other standard cam and groove couplings.

## FAQ

**Which adapter do I need?**

Check what's on the other end: threaded pipe needs type A (male) or B (female) with NPT/BSPT threads; hose needs type C/E hose shank; flanges need a flange adapter.

**Can adapters change camlock size?**

Yes — reducers connect a larger coupling to a smaller hose or pipe. Always size the camlock to the LARGER bore.

**Are adapters compatible across brands?**

Yes. Cam and groove fittings follow the A-A-59326D profile, so CamlockPro adapters mate with Dixon, Banjo, PT and other standard camlocks.
