## FAQ

**What sizes are available?**

Aluminum camlocks are stocked from 1/2" to 6" nominal hose/pipe bore, with 3/4" and 1-1/4" also common. Type A through F plus DC and DP are all available in each size.

**Which gaskets come standard?**

Standard fitment is EPDM. NBR (oil/fuel), Viton (chemicals) and PTFE (aggressive acids) options are available for the same coupling body.

**Can aluminum camlocks handle potable water?**

Yes — aluminum couplings are widely used in agriculture and water transfer. For continuous potable water duty, specify food-grade gaskets.
