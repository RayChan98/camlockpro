## FAQ

**Do I need both caps and plugs?**

One DC caps a female end, one DP plugs a male end. If both halves of a hose assembly stay connected, you need one of each.

**Are dust caps leak-proof?**

They seal against drips and dust when the coupling is not in use — they are not rated for working pressure.

**What materials?**

Aluminum, stainless steel, brass and polypropylene to match your coupling body and duty.
