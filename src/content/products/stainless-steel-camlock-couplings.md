## FAQ

**304 or 316?**

304 covers most food, water and mild chemical duty. 316 adds molybdenum for chloride and marine environments, acids and saltwater — specify 316 for coastal or aggressive media.

**Is stainless food-safe?**

Yes. 304/316 stainless with food-grade EPDM or Viton gaskets is the standard choice for dairy, beverage and edible oil lines.

**Do stainless camlocks cost much more?**

Roughly 2.5-4x aluminum per piece, but the corrosion life and reusability in food/chemical plants usually pay it back quickly.
