## FAQ

**How often should gaskets be replaced?**

Inspect at every disconnect. Soft, cracked or flattened gaskets leak — replace before a spill, not after.

**Are gaskets size-specific?**

Yes. A 2" camlock gasket fits only 2" camlocks. Order by coupling size and material compatibility.

**Can I upgrade my gasket material?**

Yes — any standard camlock accepts any of the four gasket materials. Swapping in Viton or PTFE upgrades chemical resistance without changing the coupling.
