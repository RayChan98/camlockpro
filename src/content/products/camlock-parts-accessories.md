## FAQ

**Can I rebuild a worn coupling?**

Yes — lever arm assemblies and cams are replaceable. If the body or groove is scored, replace the whole coupling.

**Do spare parts fit Dixon/Banjo camlocks?**

Standard A-A-59326D profile parts are interchangeable across brands, but always confirm with a sample or drawing.

**What causes camlocks to fail?**

Worn gaskets (most common), bent lever arms from over-tightening, and damaged grooves from dragging on the ground. Inspect both halves each use.
